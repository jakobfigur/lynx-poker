# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Research preview

Initial public snapshot of the October–November 2026 Lynx experiments.

- Visual PokerTH collector, hand reconstruction and independent log comparison.
- No-Limit Hold'em simulator, policy agents, training and evaluation tools.
- Small model starter pack with historical Lynx generations and human-style policies.
- English and German introductions, architecture, case study and generated references.
- Windows/Linux CI, contribution guidelines and security reporting policy.

The preview is experimental. Historical benchmark results are documented research observations, not a guarantee of strength or live performance.
